Crossref rate-limited three requests (UcTCRdb, OTS and Landry); exact-DOI Europe PMC records supplied the verified fields. TCR-BERT has a database title typo; the publisher API confirms the manuscript wording. No DOI or result was invented.

The dated Europe PMC searches cover gamma-delta pairing/generation, TCR language models/benchmarks and similarity/leakage benchmarks through 2026-10-06. This was a targeted update, not an exhaustive systematic review. Query strings, returned abstracts, hit counts and request metadata are cached under `reference_metadata/revision_bmc/`. The latest 100 records were retrieved for each broad search; hits outside this limit were not claimed to have been screened.

Two relevant additions were selected after abstract screening: DTCR (2026, formal journal article), an epitope-conditioned generation task; and the germline-shortcut retrieval study (2026, explicitly a preprint), supporting attention to classical alignment and exposure controls. Neither is treated as a direct gamma-delta partner-recovery performance baseline.

Current official BMC Bioinformatics submission guidelines were retrieved from `https://link.springer.com/journal/12859/submission-guidelines`; the legacy BMC URL redirected to the journal home page. The cached current page confirms double-line spacing, line/page numbers, editable TeX, no forced page breaks, figure-title/legend limits and the 20 MB additional-file limit.

The search must be refreshed again if actual submission occurs substantially later. Public archiving and author declarations remain pending separately.
